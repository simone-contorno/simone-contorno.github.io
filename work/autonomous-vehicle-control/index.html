Nonlinear MPC for Autonomous Vehicles

MyNMPC is the C++ and ROS 2 implementation developed for my master’s research at LS2N through EMARO+.

Research Details

Period
2023
Institutions
LS2N, University of Genoa, École Centrale de Nantes
Repository
MyNMPC
Stack
C++, ROS 2 Foxy, Eigen, ProxQP

Context and Problem

The research studied nonlinear model predictive control for vehicle motion: using a model to predict a trajectory and choose control inputs under constraints.

My Contribution

This was my master’s research project. I worked on the NMPC formulation and its C++/ROS 2 implementation, and documented the method and experiments in the thesis.

Technical Approach

The implementation has three main layers:

  • Model: vehicle dynamics and their derivatives.
  • MPC: the optimisation problem, solved through Sequential Quadratic Programming (SQP) with ProxQP handling the quadratic subproblems.
  • Controller: the ROS 2 interface connecting the optimiser to the robot.

The code includes different vehicle models and examples for simulation and ROSbot. Eigen is used for the matrix operations.

Results and Evidence

The research produced the MyNMPC implementation and the thesis, Nonlinear Model Predictive Control for Self-Driving Vehicles. I integrated and tested the controller on ROSbot 2R and Renault Zoe, alongside simulation-based evaluation. The thesis records the method and experimental conditions.

Research Environment

The implementation and examples target ROS 2 Foxy. The repository preserves the software used in the 2023 research.
