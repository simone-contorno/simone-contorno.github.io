Scope of the note

A guided reading of the public MyNMPC code, organised around its model, optimisation and controller layers. This outline describes a reading path; it does not report a new experiment.

1. Start with the model

Identify the state and control variables, the dynamics and the derivatives used by the optimiser. Trace how the model interface connects to the MPC layer rather than treating the ROS node as the whole controller.

2. Follow one optimisation cycle

Map the sequential quadratic programming loop to the quadratic subproblems handled by ProxQP. The complete note should point to exact source revisions and explain which quantities are updated between iterations.

3. Read the integration boundary

Follow the controller’s inputs and outputs through the ROS 2 examples. Distinguish algorithm assumptions from the configuration and timing of a particular integration.

4. Return to the research evidence

Use the thesis to establish the experimental conditions before discussing performance. Keep implementation observations separate from measurements reported in the research.

Before this becomes an article

  • Pin the source revision and annotate a complete call path.
  • Check the explanation against the thesis formulation.
  • Identify the platform and conditions for any cited result.
